# Starting out

This channel is for the questions you think are too basic. They are not, and veterans are guests in here.

**The first twenty minutes**
Tap to attack. Clear a floor, keep the gold, buy the next thing. Every zone ends in a boss that hits back harder than the floors before it, and the first one is meant to stop you.

When it does, you are not stuck. You are being told to summon.

**Reawakening is the real loop, and it happens sooner than you think**
Reawakening resets your floors, your gold, your trained stats and your essence, and pays you Relics for how deep you got. Relics buy permanent nodes that make the next climb faster than the last one.

**Do it the first time it is offered.** Everybody hesitates. Every single person who hesitated wishes they had not. Your heroes, stars, prestige, talent and gems all survive it. Only the climb resets.

## The questions that get asked every day

**"Should I save my gems?"**
Only for a featured banner you actually want. Sitting on gems does nothing on its own, and a squad you have now compounds for the rest of the run.

**"I pulled a duplicate. Is that wasted?"**
No. Duplicates are how prestige happens. Take copies of one hero into the Synthesis Chamber and stake them: more copies staked, better odds, ten is guaranteed. A failed attempt still pays star shards, so nothing is ever burned for nothing.

**"What is talent?"**
A hidden roll on every hero you pull, from bad to Mythic. It multiplies that one hero and nothing else. A high-talent 4 star can genuinely beat a low-talent 5 star, which is why the same hero is worth pulling twice.

**"My roster is full."**
Delete heroes you will not use, from the hero detail pane. You get star shards back at exactly the rate an overflowed pull would have paid, so deleting and re-pulling costs you nothing. **Lock** the ones you care about first, so nothing goes in a bulk delete by accident.

You can also buy more slots if you would rather keep everything.

**"Level a hero or Reawaken?"**
Reawaken. Levels are cheap early and get expensive fast; the tree does not. When a level costs more than it feels worth, that is the game telling you to reset.

**"Why is the gold gate limited?"**
Because gold income has no ceiling and the draws would not either. It refills every day.

## Free things to do before you spend anything

· **Daily quests.** Small, quick, and they pay gems.
· **The streak calendar.** Log in on consecutive days and it pays much better at day 7 and day 28 than it does at day 2.
· **The free daily pull** on the standard gate.
· **The hourly chest.**
· **Offline progress.** You keep earning while the game is closed. Training the right stat raises how long that keeps counting for.
· **Codes.** All of them are in #codes and most of them expire.

**The weekly race** counts floors you climb past your own frontier, so re-killing a floor you have already beaten moves nothing. It resets Monday and the winner gets a role in this server.

**Still stuck?** Ask here. Say what floor you are on and what your squad looks like, and somebody will tell you what you are missing. Screenshots help more than descriptions.
