# Read this before you trade

**One post per offer. Close your own post when the trade is done.** Tag it with what you are offering so people can browse.

**The shape of a good post**

> **Offering:** Ashen Sovereign, 5 star, 812 talent, Prestige 2
> **Looking for:** any 6 star, or a fire unit with high talent
> **Also have:** gold and essence to sweeten it
> **Timezone:** GMT, evenings

Say what you want. "Offers?" gets three people asking what you want and no trades.

%%SPLIT%%
## Not getting scammed

The trade window in game is safe. It holds both sides, shows you exactly what you are getting, and needs both people to confirm. **Everything that goes wrong happens outside it.**

**Never send first.** Not to a "trusted" trader, not to somebody with a nice role, not for a "test". If they will not use the trade window, there is a reason.

**There are no middlemen here.** Nobody is authorised to hold items for a trade. Anyone offering is stealing them.

**Nobody on the team will DM you first** about a trade, a verification, a reward or your account. Staff colours can be copied. Check the member list before you believe a name.

**Never share your password or a one-time code.** There is no trade that needs them.

**Read the confirm sheet.** It lists every hero and every coin moving in both directions. People swap an item at the last second and hope you tap through. Take the extra two seconds.

**We cannot reverse a scam.** From the server's side you confirmed a trade you meant to make, and there is no record that says otherwise. This is the reason for every line above.

**Report scammers.** Screenshot the chat and the trade, and send it to a mod. Repeat offenders get banned here and reported to Roblox, which is what actually stops them.

%%SPLIT%%
## What can and cannot move

Heroes move, and they keep their level, prestige and talent. Gold and essence can ride along. **Gems never move**, so anybody offering to "buy" a hero for gems is describing a trade the game cannot do.

**Copies you have banked do not travel.** They turn into star shards for the person giving them away, and the confirm sheet says so. Nobody is being cheated when that happens.

Both accounts need to be at least a day old, and there is a daily limit on how many trades you can make.

**Prices are whatever two people agree on.** There is no official value list, nobody here speaks for one, and anybody insisting your hero is "worth" a particular thing is negotiating, not informing.
