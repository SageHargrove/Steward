# Pick what you want to hear about

**Open `Channels & Roles` at the very top of the channel list**, above the channel names. Everything below is in there, under *Which pings do you want?*

Five pings, **none of them compulsory**. Take the ones you want, drop them any time, and take them again later. Nothing is recorded and nobody notices.

Without them, every announcement is either an @everyone that annoys most of the server or a message that most of the server never sees. That is the whole reason these exist.

**@Code Alerts**
A new code is live. This is the one worth having: most codes expire, and the generous ones expire fastest.
*Roughly a few times a month, more around updates.*

**@Update Alerts**
A new version is out, with notes in #update-notes.
*Often at first, then settling down.*

**@Banner Alerts**
A featured summon banner has opened or is about to close.
*Whenever a banner rotates.*

**@Event Alerts**
An event window has started. These are time-limited by definition, so the ping is most of the value.
*A handful a month.*

**@Giveaway Alerts**
A giveaway has started in #giveaways.
*Occasional.*

**Interest roles** are in the same place, under *What brings you to {{game}}?*. They do not ping you; they just show what you are here for.

**Roles you earn**, like @Sovereign Owner and @Weekly #1, are not in there. Those are given out. #start-here lists what they are and how they land.
