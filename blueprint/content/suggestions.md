# How suggestions work here

**Post the problem, not only the fix you thought of.** The fix you have in mind might not be the one that ships, but the annoyance behind it is the useful part, and it is exactly the part that gets lost when a post is just "add X".

**A good suggestion has**
· What you were doing when it bothered you
· What made it annoying, specifically
· Where you were, roughly. Floor, rank, how long you have played
· What you would do about it, if you have an idea

**Voting**
React 👍 on the first message of a post. That is the count that gets read. Replying "this" or "+1" does not add a vote and it buries the discussion.

**Search first.** A duplicate splits the vote, so your idea ends up looking less popular than it is.

**One idea per post.** A post with four ideas cannot be voted on, tagged or answered.

**About balance suggestions**

They are welcome and they are most of this forum. The ones that get acted on say what you were actually doing and what stopped being fun. "Essence is too tight" is a feeling. "I hit a level gate at floor 60, had nothing to spend gold on for forty minutes, and closed the game" is something that can be fixed.

Numbers get checked against what everyone else is doing, not just the person asking. If a change lands differently from what you suggested, that is usually why.

**The staff tags mean**
**under-consideration**, it is being thought about seriously. **planned**, it is happening and the post stays open until it does. **shipped**, it is in the game now. **not-planned**, with a reason, which is not the same as a bad idea and often means it collides with something you cannot see yet.

**Silence is not rejection.** It usually means it is interesting and nobody has decided yet.

**A suggestion that loosens the trading rules will be turned down**, so it is worth knowing before you write one. Every restriction in there exists because of a specific way people get robbed, and that is not a trade worth making for convenience.
