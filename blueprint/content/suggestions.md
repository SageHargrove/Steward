# How suggestions work here

Post the problem you hit, not only the solution you thought of. The fix you have in mind might not be the one that ships, but the annoyance behind it is the useful part and it is the part that gets lost when a post is only "add X".

**A good suggestion has**
· What you were doing when it bothered you
· What made it annoying, specifically
· What you would do about it, if you have an idea

**Voting**
React with 👍 on the first message of a post. That is the count that gets read. Replying "this" or "+1" does not add a vote and buries the discussion.

**Search first.** A duplicate splits the vote, so your idea ends up looking less popular than it is.

**One idea per post.** A post with four ideas cannot be voted on, tagged, or answered.

**The staff tags mean**
**under-consideration**, it is being thought about seriously. **planned**, it is going to happen and the post stays open until it does. **shipped**, it is in a build. **not-planned**, with a reason, which is not the same as a bad idea and often means it conflicts with something you cannot see yet.

Silence on a post is not rejection. It usually means it is interesting and nobody has decided yet.
