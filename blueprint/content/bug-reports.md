# How to report a bug

A good report gets fixed. A vague one gets a follow-up question and then sits for a week. The difference is about a minute of your time.

**Put these in every report**

**1. Your Roblox username.** Not your display name. Without it, anything involving your save, your currency or your heroes cannot be checked at all.
**2. What device.** Phone, tablet, PC or console, and whether you were in landscape or portrait. A surprising share of bugs are one screen shape only.
**3. What you did.** Step by step, starting from something somebody else can repeat. "Opened HEROES, sorted by talent, tapped the third one" beats "it broke".
**4. What you expected.**
**5. What happened instead.** A screenshot if it is visual, a clip if it moves. Exact wording of any error message.
**6. Roughly when.** Time and timezone, or "about ten minutes ago". Server logs are searched by time.

%%SPLIT%%
**Before you post**

**Rejoin once and see if it survives.** Plenty of visual bugs are one bad load and knowing which kind it is saves everyone a round trip.

**Search the forum.** If somebody already reported it, add what you know to their post instead of opening a new one. Two half reports are worse than one complete one, because neither has enough to act on.

**One bug per post.** Three bugs in one thread means two get forgotten.

**Tag it.** Untagged posts get tagged by hand later, which is slower for you.

**What the staff tags mean**
**confirmed**, it is reproduced and queued. **needs-info**, it is waiting on you. **fixed**, the next update should sort it. **cannot-reproduce**, we tried and could not, which is a request for more detail rather than a brush-off.

**If it cost you something**
Say exactly what, and when. Gems, gold, a hero, a purchase that did not arrive. Purchases are logged permanently on our side and can be checked and honoured. Say so plainly rather than burying it in the middle of a paragraph.

**Found an exploit?** Post it here rather than using it, and say if you would rather not describe it in public. Reporting one is looked after. Using one is rule 13.
