# You are a playtester

Thank you. Genuinely. The time you put into this is the reason the game is any good, and none of it goes unread.

**The three things worth telling us**
· **Where you got confused.** Confusion is the single most valuable thing you can report and the thing you are most likely to write off as your own fault. It is not.
· **Where you stopped, and why.** "I got bored around twenty minutes in" is worth more than a page of feature requests.
· **The moment something felt good.** Knowing what to protect matters as much as knowing what to fix.

**Watch the first ten minutes especially closely.** That is where a new player decides whether to stay. If you can remember what you were confused about at floor 5, say it before you forget, because you will forget within an hour of understanding it.

%%SPLIT%%
**Where things go**
Reactions, half-thoughts and "that felt weird" go here, in chat. Anything you can make happen twice goes in #bug-reports, because things posted here scroll away and things posted there get tracked.

**What not to worry about**
Placeholder art, missing sounds, silent music, and screens that are obviously unfinished. If it looks like it was never finished, it probably was not. Ask in here before writing it up.

**What is worth checking on your phone.** Most players are on one. If something is unreadable or unreachable on a phone in landscape, that is a real bug and not a small one.

**Say what you actually think.** Test builds are unfinished by definition, nothing you say in here gets taken personally, and the polite version of your opinion is less useful than the real one. If it feels bad, say it feels bad.
