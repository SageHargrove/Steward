# {{game}} Server Rules

Read these once. They exist so this stays somewhere people want to be.

**1 · Be decent to each other**
Disagreement is fine, contempt is not. Banter between friends is welcome, but if it lands as malicious for the person receiving it, it was. Defuse it or bring in a mod.

**2 · No hate speech. Zero tolerance.**
Slurs and bigotry of any kind, including racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia and ableism, mean an immediate permanent ban. There is no warning for this one. Your username, avatar and bio count as messages.

**3 · No NSFW or shock content**
No explicit imagery, no gore, no sexual content, anywhere, including your profile. Immediate permanent ban.

**4 · Leave politics and religion at the door**
Not because they do not matter, but because this is not the place for them. That includes the memes and the "I was only joking" versions.

**5 · Protect personal information**
Yours and everyone else's. No addresses, phone numbers, emails, real names, schools or workplaces. Check your screenshots before you post them.

**6 · Nobody on the team will DM you first**
Not the dev, not a mod, not a bot. If someone with a staff colour messages you first about a reward, a giveaway, a beta, a "verification" or your account, it is a scam. Screenshot it and tell a mod.

Nobody here will ever ask for your password, your one-time code, or access to your account. There is no reason for anyone to need it, and an account you hand over is an account you have given away.

**7 · Trades are at your own risk**
#trading is a place to find people. It is not an escrow service and nobody in it is vetted. Do the trade in game, in the trade window, where the server holds both sides and both people have to confirm. Never send first and never use a "middleman". Nothing lost to a scam can be restored, because from the server's side you gave it away on purpose.

%%SPLIT%%
**8 · No advertising, no begging**
No invite links to other servers, no promoting products or services, and never ask members for gems, Robux, gamepasses or money. Your own clips, screenshots and videos of {{game}} are not advertising and are very welcome. Invite links for a group playing {{game}} are allowed in #play-together and nowhere else.

**9 · Use the right channel**
Each one has a job, and posting in the right place is the difference between an answer and silence. Every channel says what it is for in the description at the top.

**10 · Bug reports need detail**
What you did, what you expected, what happened instead, and what device you were on. Use #bug-reports and search it first. A bug reported in general chat is a bug that gets lost.

**11 · No spam or mass pings**
No flooding, no repeated messages, no walls of images. Do not ping @everyone, the dev, staff or other members over and over, and that includes DMs to people you met here.

**12 · Do not impersonate staff or the dev**
No lookalike usernames, no copied avatars, no pretending to speak for the team. Rule 6 is why this one is taken seriously.

%%SPLIT%%
**13 · Exploits get reported, not used**
Do not use exploits, dupes, auto-clickers or third-party programs against the game, and do not share them here. Doing it costs you both sides: exploited progress gets rolled back, the account gets banned from the experience, and the Discord ban follows. Found something broken and profitable? Report it in #bug-reports and you will be looked after for it.

**14 · Credit art that is not yours**
Do not post work you have no right to post, and credit the artist when you share someone else's. If you present something as your own, it should be your own.

**15 · Listen to moderators**
Follow staff instructions in the moment and argue afterwards, in DMs, not in public chat. If you think a call was wrong, say so to a mod directly and it gets looked at properly.

**16 · Discord's rules apply, and so does common sense**
You must be 13 or older to be here. Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines apply on top of everything above. Not everything can be written down, so if you are wondering whether you should post something, that hesitation is usually the answer.
<https://discord.com/terms> · <https://discord.com/guidelines>

## How this is enforced

Most things are a conversation, not a ban.

**First strike.** A warning, logged, usually with a short note on what to do differently.
**Second strike.** A longer warning and a timeout if the situation needs room to cool down.
**Third strike.** A ban, with the length depending on what happened.

**Timeouts are not punishment.** They end an argument that is going nowhere, stop a spam wave, or give someone a chance to come back calmer.

**Some things skip the ladder** and are a permanent ban on the first offence:
· Hate speech or slurs
· NSFW or gore
· Scams, phishing, or malicious files
· Selling or advertising exploits
· Anything that threatens another member's safety

Staff judge severity case by case and may act on something not written here. These rules will change as the community grows, and changes get posted in #announcements.

**If something makes you uncomfortable** and you would rather not say so in public, DM a moderator. That is what we are for.
