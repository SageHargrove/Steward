# {{game}} — Server Rules

Read once, takes two minutes. These exist so this stays somewhere people want to be.

**1 · Be decent to each other**
Disagreement is fine, contempt is not. Banter between friends is welcome, but if it lands as malicious for the person receiving it, it was. Defuse things or bring in a mod. Do not escalate and do not pile on.

**2 · No hate speech. Zero tolerance.**
Slurs and bigotry of any kind, including racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia and ableism, mean an immediate permanent ban. This is not a warning-track offence. Usernames, avatars and bios count as messages.

**3 · No NSFW or shock content**
No explicit imagery, no gore, no sexual content, anywhere, including in your profile. Immediate permanent ban.

**4 · Leave politics and religion at the door**
Not because they do not matter, but because this is not the place for them. That includes debates, memes, and the "I was only joking" versions.

**5 · Protect personal information**
Yours and everyone else's. No addresses, phone numbers, emails, real names or workplaces, and check your screenshots before posting them.

**6 · No advertising**
No invite links to other servers, no promoting products or services, and never ask members for money. Sharing your own {{game}} clips, screenshots and videos is not advertising and is actively encouraged.
Looking for people to play with belongs in #looking-for-guild.

<!-- split -->
**7 · Use the right channel**
Each one has a job. Posting in the right place is the difference between an answer and silence.
· **#general** — anything {{game}}
· **#hero-showcase** — pulls, builds, ascensions
· **#screenshots** — what your run looked like
· **#tower-talk** — strategy, floor progression, the wall you are stuck on
· **#looking-for-guild** — recruiting and being recruited
· **#bug-reports** — one bug per post, with a build number
· **#suggestions** — one idea per post
· **#ask-the-dev** — questions, browsable afterwards
· **#off-topic** — everything that is not {{game}}
· **#other-games** — what else you are playing

**8 · Bug reports need three things**
Build number, what you did step by step, and what you expected instead. A bug reported in #general is a bug that gets lost. Search the forum first; a duplicate splits the discussion.

**9 · Playtest builds are confidential**
Anything in #playtest-lounge is unreleased. No screenshots, no footage, no files, nowhere else, until that build is public. This is the single rule most likely to cost you your @Playtester role, and it is not personal: an unfinished build shown out of context is genuinely damaging.

**10 · No spam or mass pings**
No flooding, no repeated content, no walls of images, and do not ping @everyone, staff, or other members repeatedly. That includes DMs to members you met here.

<!-- split -->
**11 · Do not impersonate staff or developers**
No lookalike usernames, no copied avatars, no pretending to speak for the team. Staff will never DM you first asking for anything.

**12 · Cheats, mods and exploits**
Talking about mods and single-player tinkering is fine. What is not fine: distributing hacks or unsafe files, using anything that affects other players' experience in guilds, raids, trade or the arena, or showing off illegitimately obtained rewards. Respect the time other people put into earning things.
Found an exploit? Report it in #bug-reports rather than using it.

**13 · Art, and what counts as yours**
Credit anything that is not your own work, and do not post art you have no right to post.
Worth being clear, since {{game}} generates art at runtime: images the game made during your run are game content and belong in #screenshots or #hero-showcase like anything else. Fan art posted as *your own drawing* should actually be your own drawing.

**14 · Listen to moderators**
Follow staff instructions in the moment; argue afterwards, not during. If you think a decision was wrong, DM a @Mod or open a post in #ask-the-dev. Do not relitigate it in public chat.

**15 · Use common sense**
Not everything can be written down. If you are wondering whether you should post something, that hesitation is usually the answer.

**16 · Discord's own rules apply**
You must be 13 or older, and Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines apply here on top of everything above.
<https://discord.com/terms> · <https://discord.com/guidelines>

## How this is enforced

Most things are a conversation, not a ban. The ladder:

**First strike** — a warning, logged, and usually a short explanation of what to do differently.
**Second strike** — a longer warning and a timeout, if the situation needs the room to cool down.
**Third strike** — a ban, with the length depending on what happened.

**Timeouts** are not punishment. They are used to end an argument that is going nowhere, stop a spam wave, or give someone a chance to come back calmer.

**Some things skip the ladder entirely** and are an immediate permanent ban on a first offence:
· Hate speech or slurs
· NSFW or gore
· Scams, phishing, or malicious files
· Anything that threatens another member's safety

Staff decide severity case by case and reserve the right to act on something not written here. These rules will change as the community grows, and changes get posted in #announcements.

If something makes you uncomfortable and you would rather not say so publicly, DM a @Mod. That is what we are for.
