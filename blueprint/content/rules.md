# {{game}} Server Rules

Please take a moment to review the rules. These exist so this stays somewhere people want to be.

**1 · Be decent to each other**
Disagreement is fine, contempt is not. Banter between friends is welcome, but if it lands as malicious for the person receiving it, it was. Defuse things or bring in a mod.

**2 · No hate speech. Zero tolerance.**
Slurs and bigotry of any kind, including racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia and ableism, mean an immediate permanent ban. This is not a warning-track offence. Usernames, avatars and bios count as messages.

**3 · No NSFW or shock content**
No explicit imagery, no gore, no sexual content, anywhere, including in your profile. Immediate permanent ban.

**4 · Leave politics and religion at the door**
Not because they do not matter, but because this is not the place for them. That includes debates, memes, and the "I was only joking" versions.

**5 · Protect personal information**
Yours and everyone else's. No addresses, phone numbers, emails, real names or workplaces, and check your screenshots before posting them.

**6 · No advertising**
No invite links to other servers, no promoting products or services, and never ask members for money. Sharing your own clips, screenshots and videos of {{game}} is not advertising and is actively encouraged.

**7 · Use the right channel**
Each one has a job, and posting in the right place is the difference between an answer and silence. Check the channel list; most of them say what they are for in the description at the top.

**8 · Bug reports need three things**
What you did step by step, what you expected, and what happened instead. A version or build number if you can find one. A bug reported in general chat is a bug that gets lost, so use the bug channel and search it first, because a duplicate splits the discussion.

**9 · No spam or mass pings**
No flooding, no repeated content, no walls of images. Do not ping @everyone, staff, or other members repeatedly, and that includes DMs to people you met here.

**10 · Do not impersonate staff or developers**
No lookalike usernames, no copied avatars, no pretending to speak for the team. Staff will never DM you first asking for anything.

**11 · Cheats, mods and exploits**
Talking about mods and single-player tinkering is fine. What is not: distributing hacks or unsafe files, using anything that affects other players, or showing off illegitimately obtained rewards. Respect the time other people put into earning things. Found an exploit? Report it rather than using it.

**12 · Credit art that is not yours**
Do not post work you have no right to post, and credit the artist when you share someone else's. If you are presenting something as your own, it should be your own.

**13 · Mark spoilers**
Use Discord's spoiler tags for anything story-related that people might not have reached yet. Wrap it in two pairs of bars, written as `||like this||`, and it stays hidden until someone chooses to click.

**14 · Listen to moderators**
Follow staff instructions in the moment; argue afterwards, not during. If you think a decision was wrong, DM a moderator. Do not relitigate it in public chat.

**15 · Use common sense**
Not everything can be written down. If you are wondering whether you should post something, that hesitation is usually the answer.

**16 · Discord's own rules apply**
You must be 13 or older, and Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines apply here on top of everything above.
<https://discord.com/terms> · <https://discord.com/guidelines>

## How this is enforced

Most things are a conversation, not a ban. The ladder:

**First strike.** A warning, logged, usually with a short explanation of what to do differently.
**Second strike.** A longer warning and a timeout, if the situation needs room to cool down.
**Third strike.** A ban, with the length depending on what happened.

**Timeouts are not punishment.** They end an argument that is going nowhere, stop a spam wave, or give someone the chance to come back calmer.

**Some things skip the ladder** and are an immediate permanent ban on a first offence:
· Hate speech or slurs
· NSFW or gore
· Scams, phishing, or malicious files
· Anything that threatens another member's safety

Staff judge severity case by case and may act on something not written here. These rules will change as the community grows, and changes get posted in the announcements channel.

If something makes you uncomfortable and you would rather not say so publicly, DM a moderator. That is what we are for.
