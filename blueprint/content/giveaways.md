# How giveaways work

Gems, gamepasses and the occasional oddity. Everything here is run by the team, and everything real is announced in this channel.

**How to enter**
React or press the button on the giveaway message. That is all. There is nothing to buy, nothing to install and nobody to message.

**How winners are told**
A winner is announced **in this channel**, publicly, in a reply to the giveaway itself. You will be told here first.

**Then you come to us.** A winner messages a mod to claim, not the other way around.

%%SPLIT%%
## The part that matters

**Nobody on the team will DM you first.** Not to tell you that you won, not to "verify" you, not to send a link, not to ask for anything. Every message like that is a scam, including the ones that get your username right.

**A real giveaway never needs:**
· Your password, or a one-time code
· You to join another server
· You to buy a gamepass first, or send anything first
· You to click a link to "claim"

**Report it.** Screenshot the DM and send it to a mod. It takes you thirty seconds and it stops the same person going down the member list.

## House rules

**One entry per person.** Alt accounts are the fastest way to be barred from every future giveaway, and they are easier to spot than people think.

**Be in the server when it ends.** A winner who has left gets rerolled, immediately and without ceremony.

**Claim within 48 hours** or it gets rerolled. Nothing personal, it just cannot sit open forever.

**Take @Giveaway Alerts** in #pick-your-roles if you want a ping when one starts. Without it you will find out afterwards, which is the same as not entering.
