# Welcome to {{game}}

{{game}} is a tower-climbing gacha where the heroes are nameless until the world remembers them. This server is where it gets made in public: builds, bugs, arguments about floor 40, and the occasional screenshot of a pull nobody believes.

**Start here**
· Read #rules. Two minutes, and the short version is "be decent".
· Say hello in #general. First post is the hardest one.
· Answer the questions when you joined? Those set your roles. You can change them any time from **Channels & Roles** at the top of the channel list.

**Where things happen**
· #announcements — releases, playtests, events. Worth turning on.
· #patch-notes — every build, separately, so you can mute one and not the other.
· #devlog — how it is being made, and why things are the way they are.
· #hero-showcase — pulls and builds. The busiest channel here, reliably.
· #tower-talk — strategy and whatever wall you are stuck on.
· #bug-reports — one bug per post, with a build number.
· #suggestions — one idea per post. Vote with 👍 rather than posting "this".
· #ask-the-dev — questions get answered and stay searchable.

**Roles you can pick up**
· **@Ping Me For Playtests** — a mention when a build goes out. A few times a month at most, and you can drop it whenever. This is the only way we reach you; nobody here will DM you about a playtest.
· **@Tower Climber**, **@Guild Seeker**, **@Bug Hunter**, **@Lore Reader** — pick whichever fits, they just show what you are here for.
Grab or drop any of them under **Channels & Roles**.

**Roles you earn**
· **@Founding Member** — you were here before the public announcement. Granted by hand and then never again.
· **@Playtester** — access to #playtest-lounge and early builds. Ask if you want in.
· **@Veteran** — around long enough to be part of the furniture.

**A note on what is recorded**
A bot logs who posted in which channel and when, so that questions like "did anyone come back after the last build" have an answer. It does **not** record what anyone writes; it does not even ask Discord for permission to read message content.
Run `/my-data` to see exactly what is stored about you, and `/forget-me` to erase it and stop it happening. No explanation needed.

Glad you are here. Go break something and tell us about it.
