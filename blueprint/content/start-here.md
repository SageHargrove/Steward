# Welcome to {{game}}

{{tagline}}

You start at floor 1 with nothing. E rank, no squad, no gold, and a System window only you can see. Everything after that you build.

This server is where the people climbing it talk, and where the game gets made in the open.

**Do these three things**
**1.** Read #rules. The short version is: be decent to people.
**2.** Pick your pings in #pick-your-roles. Nothing there is compulsory and you can drop any of them later.
**3.** Say hello in #general.

**Free gems before you go anywhere else**
#codes has every working code and every dead one, so check there before asking. To redeem, tap **OPTIONS** in game, scroll to the bottom, and type it into **REDEEM A CODE**.

Codes are posted there the moment they go live, and most of them expire. Take the **@Code Alerts** ping if you want to catch them.

## Where things happen

**Read these**
· **#announcements** . Updates, events, and anything worth stopping for.
· **#update-notes** . What changed in each version, down to the small stuff.
· **#codes** . Working codes, and the ones that have run out.
· **#hall-of-fame** . The weekly winner and the all-time board.

**Post in these**
· **#general** . Everything about {{game}}.
· **#new-climbers** . Starting out and stuck. No question is too basic in here, and veterans are guests.
· **#media** . Your pulls, your screenshots, your clips. Post the 7 star.
· **#fan-art** . Art of the game, by anyone. Credit the artist if it is not yours.
· **#play-together** . Find people to play alongside.
· **#trading** . One post per offer. Read the pinned post first, it is mostly about not getting scammed.
· **#giveaways** . Gem and gamepass drops. Anything that asks you to DM first is a scam.
· **#off-topic** . Everything that is not {{game}}.

**Tell us things in these**
· **#bug-reports** . One bug per post. There is a pinned post showing what to include.
· **#suggestions** . One idea per post. Vote with 👍 instead of replying "this".
· **#ask-the-dev** . Ask anything. Answers stay searchable, so check before you post.
· **#devlog** . How the game gets made, and why things ended up the way they did.

## Roles

**Pings you choose.** All of them live in #pick-your-roles and none of them are required.
· **@Code Alerts** . A new code is live.
· **@Update Alerts** . A new version is out.
· **@Banner Alerts** . A featured summon banner has changed.
· **@Event Alerts** . An event window has opened.
· **@Giveaway Alerts** . A giveaway has started.

**Roles you earn.** These are handed out, not requested.
· **@Founding Member** . You were here before the public launch. Granted once, then never again.
· **@Sovereign Owner** . You pulled a 7 star. Post the proof in #media.
· **@Rank SSS** . You reached SSS in game.
· **@Weekly #1** . You won the weekly race. It moves to somebody else on Monday.
· **@All-Time Top 10** . You are on the all-time board.
· **@Codex Complete** . Every hero collected.
· **@Apex Climber** . You reached the top floor.
· **@Playtester** . Early builds and #playtest-lounge. Ask if you want in.
· **@Veteran** . People who have been around a while and help others.

Chatting also levels you up: **Awakened** at level 5, then **Climber**, **Wallbreaker**, **Ascendant**, and **Apex** at 60. They are cosmetic and nobody is counting.

## Two housekeeping notes

**Not everyone can be here.** Discord requires you to be 13 or older and Roblox does not, so part of the playerbase cannot join this server at all. If that is you or a friend, the Roblox group is the way to keep up. Everything announced here gets posted there too.

**What gets recorded.** A bot logs who posted in which channel and when, so questions like "did people come back after the update" have an answer. It does **not** record what anyone writes. It does not even ask Discord for permission to read message content.

Run `/my-data` to see exactly what is stored about you, and `/forget-me` to erase it and stop it happening. No explanation needed and nothing is held against you for it.

Glad you are here. Go break something and tell us about it.
